Detailed Specification • Tab 2

Sheet 2: Footnote & Temperature Exception Audit

This is where generic data processors and automated AI fail catastrophically. In industrial fluid power, instrumentation, and piping, the safe operating limit is rarely the large bold headline on page 1. It is almost always conditioned by footnotes on page 6 or 8.

[VERBATIM MANUFACTURER FOOTNOTE EXTRACT]

"Ashcroft Type 1009: Standard gauge rated to 200°F (93°C) ambient. When specified with liquid glycerin fill, maximum process temperature is de-rated to 150°F (65°C). For process steam applications exceeding 150°F, an external siphon or diaphragm seal is strictly required."

Audited by Vellett: Flagged on Tab 2 with exact PDF page coordinate (Page 6, Footnote Note 3).

Every footnote is transcribed verbatim, cross-referenced against the affected SKU list, and assigned an operational warning code so your sales reps quote the required accessory siphon or PEEK seal upfront.

Detailed Specification • Tab 4

Sheet 4: Annual Price Sheet Reconciliation

Suppliers regularly issue annual price adjustments in dense, unformatted spreadsheets or multi-page PDFs. If your purchasing team does not update the ERP cost matrix before sales reps quote contracts, your distributor absorbs the price hike directly out of gross margin.

Tab 4 compares previous distributor purchase cost against new supplier list and multiplier tables, computing dollar variance, percentage delta, and flagging any price increase exceeding your preset alert threshold (e.g., >5%).

Detailed Specification • Tab 5

Sheet 5: Faceted Search & Attribute Taxonomies

On modern B2B e-commerce sites (BigCommerce, Magento, Adobe Commerce), customer search abandonment rates exceed 35% when attribute filters are fragmented. If one SKU is tagged "1/4 NPT", another "0.25 Inch NPT", and a third "1/4" Male NPT", your faceted search fails.

Tab 5 provides unified, controlled vocabularies for all technical attributes: connection sizes, pressure ranges, wetted metallurgy, dial diameters, and body configurations.
