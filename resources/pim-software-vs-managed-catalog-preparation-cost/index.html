In-House PIM Software vs. Managed Catalog Preparation: Total Cost of Ownership Comparison

An executive cost-benefit analysis comparing $60,000/year enterprise PIM licenses (Akeneo, Inriver, Salsify) that require dedicated in-house data teams against Vellett's managed turnkey catalog engineering service.

AS
Ayush Sharma
Catalog Engineering Director, Vellett

When industrial distributors realize their product data is holding back their e-commerce growth, the instinctive reaction of leadership is often: "We need to buy a PIM (Product Information Management) software."

Six months and $90,000 later, executive leadership is shocked to discover that PIM software does not write product data. A PIM is simply a database container. It provides empty fields for attributes, categories, and images. Someone still has to read 300-page engineering PDFs, unroll part matrices, verify ASME accuracy grades, calculate net multipliers, and crop 1500px images.

In this guide, we break down the true 3-year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) comparing in-house PIM implementations against Vellett's managed catalog engineering desk.
