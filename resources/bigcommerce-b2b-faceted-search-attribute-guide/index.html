Building Faceted Search Attributes for Industrial B2B E-Commerce (BigCommerce & Magento)

Why 72% of industrial buyers abandon distributor websites: the lack of parametric faceted filters. Learn how to design a standardized attribute schema (Port Size, Thread Standard, Body Material, Pressure Range, Accuracy Grade) for BigCommerce B2B Edition.

AS
Ayush Sharma
Catalog Engineering Director, Vellett

Industrial e-commerce is fundamentally different from consumer retail. A consumer searches for "men's running shoes size 10" and browses lifestyle photography. An industrial mechanical engineer or MRO buyer lands on your website with an exact engineering constraint: "1/4 inch NPT male lower mount pressure gauge, 0 to 100 psi, 316 stainless steel wetted parts, ASME Grade 1A."

If your website displays 800 unorganized pressure gauges with cryptic manufacturer part numbers and zero sidebar filters, that engineer will not scroll through 40 pages of results. They will close the tab and buy from Grainger, Motion, or McMaster-Carr—even if your price is 15% lower. According to B2B e-commerce benchmarks, 72% of industrial procurement officers abandon distributor websites due to a lack of parametric faceted search.

In this guide, we break down how to design a standardized attribute schema and configure BigCommerce B2B Edition and Adobe Commerce (Magento) to deliver frictionless parametric filtering for technical industrial catalogs.

1. The 12 Mandatory Facet Attributes for Fluid Power & Instrumentation

To satisfy an engineering buyer, your product master data must populate twelve standardized attribute fields across valves, fittings, gauges, and pneumatics:

Facet 01 Dial / Body Size e.g., 2.5 in (63 mm), 4 in, 1/4 in DN8
Facet 02 Process Connection Size e.g., 1/8", 1/4", 1/2", 3/4"
Facet 03 Thread / End Standard e.g., NPT, BSPP, BSPT, Tri-Clamp, Flanged
Facet 04 Connection Location e.g., Lower Mount (LM), Center Back (CBM)
Facet 05 Pressure Range (Min/Max) e.g., 0-30 psi, 0-100 psi, 0-3000 psi
Facet 06 Wetted Metallurgy e.g., 316L SS, Brass, Monel 400, Hastelloy C
Facet 07 Case & Enclosure Material e.g., 304 SS, Phenolic, Die Cast Aluminum
Facet 08 Accuracy Rating e.g., ASME Grade 1A (±1.0%), Grade 2A (±0.5%)
Facet 09 Case Fill / Damping e.g., Dry, Glycerin Filled, Silicone Filled

2. BigCommerce Custom Fields vs. Product Modifiers

In BigCommerce, many distributors make the mistake of creating 1 "parent product" with 500 dropdown option variants. This breaks for industrial catalogs because:

  • BigCommerce enforces a strict limit of 600 SKU variants per parent product.
  • Parent-variant configurations hide individual manufacturer part numbers from Google organic search indexing.
  • ERP synchronizers (e.g. Prophet 21 to BigCommerce via eBridge or Celigo) struggle to map multi-tiered option trees.

The professional architectural pattern is Standalone Products with BigCommerce Custom Fields. Every unique manufacturer part number is created as an independent product record, with standardized Custom Fields that power BigCommerce's Native Product Filtering:

// BigCommerce Product CSV Import Schema (Custom Fields Column) 
Product Code/SKU,Product Name,Price,Custom Fields
ASH-25-1009-SW-02L-100#,"Ashcroft 1009S 2.5 in Pressure Gauge, 0-100 psi, 1/4 NPT LM, 316L SS",92.50,"Dial Size=2.5 in (63 mm);Pressure Range=0-100 PSI;Connection Size=1/4 in NPT;Mount Location=Lower Mount;Wetted Material=316L Stainless Steel;Accuracy=Grade 1A (+/-1.0%);Fill=Dry"

3. Normalizing Numeric Ranges for Slider Filtering

To enable numeric slider filters (e.g., allowing buyers to drag a slider between 0 and 5,000 PSI), values cannot be stored as text strings like 0-100 psi. They must be split into two numeric integer columns: Range_Min_PSI and Range_Max_PSI.

Vellett's catalog engine automatically extracts discrete numeric minimums and maximums, converting bar, kPa, and in. w.c. into dual-unit attributes so customers can filter in either imperial or metric scales effortlessly.
