The 5-Day Supplier Onboarding Framework: From Messy PDF to Live ERP & E-Commerce

Vellett's battle-tested 5-day protocol for taking a new 500-SKU manufacturer catalog from a dense engineering PDF to a 100% verified production import file inside Prophet 21 and BigCommerce. Includes executive checklists for each day.

AS
Ayush Sharma
Catalog Engineering Director, Vellett

When an industrial distributor secures a new manufacturer line, the clock starts ticking immediately. Every day those products are not loaded into Prophet 21 and published to your e-commerce storefront is a day your competitors capture sales.

Yet traditional onboarding takes 3 to 6 months. In this guide, we pull back the curtain on Vellett's proprietary 5-Day Supplier Onboarding Framework—the protocol we use to convert 500 to 2,000 SKUs from a messy manufacturer PDF into live, transacting ERP items in one business week.

The 5-Day Execution Blueprint

DAY 01 PDF Extraction & OCR Audit

Deconstruction & Boundary Mapping

Extract high-resolution vector tables, cross-check page counts against the latest manufacturer bulletin revision, and harvest raw product photographs into 1500x1500px square assets.

DAY 02 Permutation Unrolling

Combinatorial Matrix Expansion

Run ordering formulas through Vellett's Python expansion engine. Enforce metallurgical and pressure dependencies to prune phantom combinations.

DAY 03 Footnote Engineering Audit

Application Limitations & Safety Catches

Extract all fine-print footnotes verbatim. Build Tab 2 Exception Log, mapping liquid-fill thresholds, steam ratings, and maximum static pressure caveats to ERP notes.

DAY 04 Pricing & Equivalents

Cross-Referencing & Margin Recalculation

Map drop-in equivalents from two competing brands (Tab 3). Reconcile supplier list prices against distributor purchase multipliers (Tab 4) to ensure 35%+ margins.

DAY 05 Production Go-Live

ERP Ingestion & Executive Sign-Off

Generate native Prophet 21 (DynaChange) and Infor CSD import files. Deliver the finalized 5-sheet master Excel deliverable with executive contrast verification (Tab 5).
