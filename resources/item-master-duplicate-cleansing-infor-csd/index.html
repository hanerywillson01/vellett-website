Item Master Cleansing for Multi-Branch Distributors: De-duplicating Infor CloudSuite Distribution (CSD)

How branch acquisitions and decentralized purchasing create massive catalog bloat in Infor CSD / SX.e. A step-by-step framework for fuzzy part number normalization (`SS-62TF4` vs `SS 62TF4`), UOM unification, and single-source-of-truth item master creation.

AS
Vellett Engineering Desk
Catalog Engineering Director, Vellett

When an industrial distributor grows by acquisition—purchasing regional competitors across the Midwest, Texas, or the Southeast—the single biggest integration obstacle is always the item master. Branch A in Houston called a 1/4" Swagelok ball valve SS-62TF4. Branch B in Dallas entered it as SS 62TF4 without hyphens. Branch C in Atlanta created it under their own internal SKU VLV-SWG-004.

Inside Infor CloudSuite Distribution (CSD / SX.e), these appear as three distinct inventory items. The consequences are crippling:

  • Fragmented Purchasing Power: Buyers place three separate orders with the factory, failing to hit master volume rebate thresholds.
  • Dead Inventory Accumulation: Branch A reorders 50 valves while Branch B has 80 units sitting unsold in a warehouse 40 miles away.
  • E-Commerce Duplicate Clutter: Your B2B website shows three duplicate listings for the same valve with conflicting photos and prices.

1. The Normalized Cleansing Algorithm

Vellett cleanses multi-branch Infor CSD catalogs using a three-stage de-duplication pipeline:

1. Alphanumeric Skeleton Normalization Strip all punctuation, slashes, spaces, and hyphens: SS-62TF4, SS 62TF4, and SS/62TF4 all collapse into the normalized key SS62TF4.
2. Manufacturer Prefix Standardization Enforce a single authoritative 3-letter manufacturer prefix (e.g. SWG- for Swagelok, ASH- for Ashcroft, WIK- for WIKA).
3. Warehouse Location Stock Consolidation Merge on-hand balances, sales history, and open purchase orders into the master SKU, safely archiving legacy duplicate items in Infor CSD.
