Six Specialized Catalog Disciplines for Industrial Distributors

From unrolling 200-page manufacturer PDF catalogs into Prophet 21 to auditing 40,000 legacy ERP SKUs for duplicate records, Vellett provides dedicated engineering capacity for your product data operations.

Principal Lead: Ayush Bijalwan • SLA Guarantee: 5-Day Sprint Delivery • Direct Desk: (919) 339-1208
Discipline 01

New Supplier PDF Catalog Onboarding

Delivery Sprint: 5 Business Days per Catalog
Format: 20-Attribute Prophet 21 / Infor CSD Master

When you sign a new distribution agreement with an instrumentation or valve manufacturer, your sales reps cannot sell until part numbers exist in your ERP. We take raw, unformatted 50 to 500-page engineering PDFs, unroll matrix code options (dial size, pressure range, mount type, wetted metallurgy), and format clean 20-attribute records ready for instant ERP upload.

Key Output: 100% normalized item master with embedded thumbnail images, HTML bullet specifications, category taxonomies, and verified PDF page citations.
Discipline 02

Drop-In Stock-Out Cross-Referencing

Target Platforms: Prophet 21 Substitute Items, Infor Interchange
Validation: Form-Fit-Function Verified

Factory lead times of 16 to 24 weeks on critical valves and instrumentation cost distributors massive counter revenue. We map every SKU from your primary supplier lines against interchangeable in-stock competitors (Swagelok ↔ Parker, Ashcroft ↔ WIKA, Dwyer ↔ Omega, Apollo ↔ Milwaukee).

Key Output: Tab 3 Cross-Reference Interchange Matrix with specific form-fit-function caveats to give counter reps immediate quoting confidence.
Discipline 03

Annual Supplier Price Sheet Reconciliation

Audit Scope: Cost Deltas, Margin Leakage, Multiplier Checks
Format: Variance Ledger with Automated Delta %

Suppliers regularly distribute price increases in messy format changes that break existing ERP import scripts. If your ERP cost tables lag, you sell at stale prices while the manufacturer bills at the higher rate, eroding 3% to 8% of distributor gross margin.

Key Output: Side-by-side delta reconciliation highlighting price increases above threshold limits, obsolete part flags, and revised multiplier tiers.
Discipline 04

ERP Item Master Duplicate Cleansing

Common Systems: Epicor Prophet 21, Infor Distribution One / SX.e
Resolution: Master SKU Merge & Cross-Reference Redirection

Decades of manual clerk entry create massive item master bloat. A single Swagelok fitting might exist under four separate part numbers in your ERP: "SS-62TS8", "SS 62TS8", "SS62TS8", and "SWG-SS-62TS8". This fragments warehouse inventory counts, forces unnecessary re-orders, and breaks e-commerce search.

Key Output: Merged canonical SKU mapping with historical purchase order preservation and automatic redirect aliases.
Discipline 05

Multi-Branch Catalog Standardization

Target Scenarios: M&A Branch Consolidation, Regional Unification
Format: Enterprise Item Master Harmonization

When distributors acquire regional supply houses, each branch arrives with its own legacy item descriptions, unit abbreviations, and category hierarchies. Branch A calls it "1/4 NPT 316SS", Branch B calls it "1/4" MNPT SS316", and Branch C has no material listed. We harmonize multi-branch catalogs into a single, corporate master.

Key Output: Standardized multi-branch item master enabling consolidated corporate purchasing and inter-branch stock transfers.
Discipline 06

Technical Footnote Exception Auditing

Risk Mitigation: Operating Limits, Media Alerts, Warranty Defense
Audit Method: Human-in-the-Loop Engineering Review

Automated scrapers and OCR tools extract high-level tables while completely ignoring fine-print footnotes at the bottom of pages. In industrial piping and process control, footnotes contain life-safety information: de-rating curves for temperature, steam limits, minimum media viscosity, and mandatory accessories (siphons, snubbers, diaphragm seals).

Key Output: Tab 2 Footnote Exception Audit capturing every operational de-rating with verbatim manufacturer citations and ERP exception flags.
